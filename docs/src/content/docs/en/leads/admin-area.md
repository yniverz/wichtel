:::tip[At a glance]

- **Get there:** in the volunteer view, tap _Admin_ in the menu (or _Go to admin_ on the home page).
- **Choose the edition:** at the top of the sidebar in the _Edition_ field, on a phone under _Menu_.
- **You only see what your role has permissions for** – and only in the areas it applies to.
- **Back:** _← Volunteer view_.
- Questions about your permissions go to your festival's admins.

:::

## Step by step

1. Log in. In the volunteer view the menu has _Admin_; the home page also shows “You have admin
   permissions.” with the button _Go to admin_.
2. You land on the _Overview_. On a computer the menu is in the sidebar on the left; on a phone you
   open it at the top right with _Menu_.
3. Check in the _Edition_ field that the right edition is selected. The current edition is marked
   “(current)”.
4. Next to every page title, the round **?** opens the matching help page in a new tab.
5. At the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone under _Menu_), _← Volunteer view_ takes you back to your
   own shifts. The language switch is there too, and on a computer also _Help_ (the start page of
   this help for leads) and _Log out_.

## The menu

The items are grouped by task. Which ones you see depends on your role's permissions (the
permission names are spelled as in the role settings):

| Group         | Menu item                             | Appears if your role …                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| –             | _Overview_                            | always                                                                                                         |
| On site       | _Desk & check-in_                     | has _Hand out goodies_ or _Confirm attendance (check-in)_, or _Adjust points manually_ for the whole edition   |
| Planning      | _Shifts_, _Requests_                  | has _Create and edit shifts_, _Add and remove volunteers, approve requests_ or _Confirm attendance (check-in)_ |
| Planning      | _Areas_                               | has _Create and edit areas_                                                                                    |
| Planning      | _Booking waves_, _Places & site plan_ | has _Create and edit shifts_ for the whole edition                                                             |
| Planning      | _Goodies_                             | has _Manage goodies_ for the whole edition                                                                     |
| People        | _People_                              | has _Assign roles (only with own permissions)_ or _View volunteer contact details_                             |
| People        | _Qualifications_                      | has _Review and grant qualifications_                                                                          |
| Communication | _News_                                | has _Publish news (also by e-mail)_                                                                            |
| System        | _Audit log_                           | has _View audit log_ for the whole edition                                                                     |

_Roles_, _Profile fields_, _Email templates_, _Delivery_, _Editions_, _Settings_ and _Privacy & legal
notice_ are only visible to admins.

A number appears next to _Requests_ as soon as booking requests or handovers are waiting for a
decision.

## Common questions

### Why is a menu item missing for me?

Your role lacks the matching permission, or it only has it for one area while the menu item needs it
for the whole edition. Your festival's admins give out permissions – ask them.

### Why don't I see all of the festival's shifts?

A role is given either for the whole edition or for one area. For an area it also applies to all of
its sub-areas, but not to other areas. Lists, the overview and print views only show you what lies
within your areas.

### I get “You do not have permission to do this.”

The page or action needs a permission your role does not have (in this area or edition). This
happens, for example, when you follow a link to a person's page but have no access to _People_.

### What does the edition at the top mean?

An edition is one festival year with its own areas, shifts and points. In the admin area you always
work on exactly one edition; your browser remembers the choice. Volunteers, on the other hand,
always see the current edition.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>Roles apply per edition</summary>

Every role is given for one particular edition. If you switch to another edition at the top, only the
roles you have in that edition apply there. Roles in an archived edition no longer give any
permissions.

Contact details and uploaded proofs are specially protected: the permissions _View volunteer contact
details_ and _View uploaded documents_ only work through roles in the **current** edition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I chose an edition and can no longer get into the admin area</summary>

If you have no role in the selected edition, every page of the admin area shows “You do not have
permission to do this.” – so the switch is out of reach too. The choice is stored in your browser.
Delete this site's cookies in your browser settings and log in again; the admin area then opens the
current edition again. So only choose editions in which you have a role.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I don't see the “Admin” menu item even though I have a role</summary>

The menu item in the volunteer view follows the current edition. If your role was given for another
edition (e.g. already for next year), it does not appear. Ask the admins.

</details>
