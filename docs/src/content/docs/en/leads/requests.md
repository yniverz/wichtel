:::tip[At a glance]

- **Open:** _Admin_ → _Requests_. The number next to the menu item shows how much is open.
- **At the top** are _Handovers and swaps_, **below** _Booking requests_.
- For each entry: _Approve_ or _Decline_. Everyone involved gets an e-mail.
- You only see requests from areas in which you may _Add and remove volunteers, approve requests_.

:::

## Booking requests

A booking request is created when someone signs up for a position _on request_ (see
[Positions](/wichtel/en/leads/positions/)). Each entry shows the person, the shift, the position and
the time. Name and shift are links to their detail pages.

- **_Approve_** – the person is signed up and gets the e-mail “Confirmed: …”. If the position has
  filled up in the meantime, you get “All places are taken.” – then make room first or decline.
- **_Decline_** – the person gets the e-mail “Not this time: …” with a link to other shifts.

You find the same buttons directly in the shift's staffing list, next to the status _Request_.

## Handovers and swaps

Volunteers can hand their shift over to someone else or swap with someone. Usually this happens
without you. A lead only has to approve if

- the position is _on request_ (then always), or
- the cancellation deadline has passed and your festival or your area is set up so that late
  handovers need approval.

An entry shows the shift, the position, the time and who hands over to whom:

- “Anna → Ben” – Anna hands her shift over to Ben.
- “Anna ↔ Ben, in exchange for …” – the two swap; Ben's shift follows.

Until you decide, everything stays as it is: the person handing over stays signed up.

- **_Approve_** – Wichtel checks once more that the shift has not started and that the person taking
  over has the required qualifications and no other shift at that time. Then the shift is rebooked.
  The person taking over gets “You are signed up: …”, the person handing over gets “Taken over: …”.
- **_Decline_** – both get “Handover did not happen: …”. Nothing changes about their shifts.

## Common questions

### Why don't I see a request that a colleague sees?

You only see requests from your areas. For a swap you have to be responsible for **both** shifts – if
they are in different areas, someone with permissions for both decides (e.g. a lead for the whole
edition).

### Can I undo a decision?

Not on this page. If you approved by mistake, remove the person from the shift again (see
[Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)). If you declined by mistake, add them yourself.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>Approving fails with an error message</summary>

Possible reasons: the shift has already started, the person handing over has already been ticked off,
the person taking over lacks a qualification, or they now have another shift at the same time. Then
you can only decline the handover.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is this recorded anywhere?</summary>

Yes. Approvals, declines, handovers and swaps are recorded in the audit log.

</details>
