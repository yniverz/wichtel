---
title: Writing news
description: Write posts for your area, a shift or the crew, send them by e-mail if you like, pin, edit and delete them.
---

:::tip[At a glance]

- **New:** _Admin_ → _News_ → _New post_ → choose _For_ → title and text → _Preview_ → _Publish_.
- **To one shift:** open the shift → _Message everyone_.
- **By e-mail:** tick _Also send by e-mail_ – it goes to everyone the post concerns right now.
- **Keep important things on top:** _Pin to the top_, optionally _Pinned up to and including_.
- Your role needs _Publish news (also by e-mail)_.

:::

News appears in the volunteers' news feed (_News_ in the app, the latest also on the home page) – but
only for the people it is meant for.

## Who you can write to

| _For_                              | Whom it reaches                                                                         | Who may choose it                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _Everyone on this shift_           | everyone signed up for the shift, with a request or a reserved place                    | only via _Message everyone_ on the shift's page; permission in the shift's area |
| _Everyone with shifts in an area_  | everyone with a shift in the area or its sub-areas (requests and reservations included) | for the areas in which you have the permission                                  |
| _All volunteers of this edition_   | everyone with at least one shift in the edition                                         | permission for the whole edition                                                |
| _Everyone with a role (crew)_      | everyone with a role in the edition                                                     | permission for the whole edition                                                |
| _Everyone with this role_          | everyone with the chosen role in the edition                                            | permission for the whole edition                                                |
| _All accounts_                     | all accounts                                                                            | admins only                                                                     |
| _Public (also without an account)_ | everyone, even without logging in                                                       | admins only                                                                     |

The form only offers what you may choose and preselects the narrowest group. Anyone who books a shift
in the area later also sees an area post then.

## Step by step

1. Open _News_ and tap _New post_ – or on a shift's page tap _Message everyone_.
2. Choose the group under _For_; for _Everyone with shifts in an area_ also the area.
3. Write _Title (German)_ and _Text (German)_. The text can use simple formatting: paragraphs with an
   empty line, `# heading`, `- list`, `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `[link text](https://…)`.
4. Optionally under _English version_: title and text in English. People who chose English then see
   and receive this version, everyone else the German one.
5. Optionally _Pin to the top_ and _Pinned up to and including_ (see below).
6. Optionally _Also send by e-mail_.
7. Tap _Preview_. You see “What volunteers will see” and “Reaches {number} people”.
8. If everything is fine, tap _Publish_ and confirm (“Publish this post and e-mail it to {number}
   people?” if the e-mail goes along).

## By e-mail

With _Also send by e-mail_, everyone the post concerns **at the moment of publishing** also gets it as
an e-mail – in their language, with a link to the post. Anyone who joins later only sees it in the
news feed. Afterwards the list shows “e-mailed to {number}”.

## Pinning

_Pin to the top_ keeps the post above all others, e.g. during a disruption. With _Pinned up to and
including_ this ends by itself at the end of the chosen day; without a date it stays pinned until you
switch it off. Pinned posts are marked _Pinned_ in the list.

## Editing and deleting

1. Tap the post in the _News_ list.
2. Change it under _Edit post_ and tap _Save_ – or tap _Delete_ and confirm.

:::note
Changes are not e-mailed again. Deleted posts disappear from everyone's news feed; an e-mail that has
already been sent cannot be recalled.
:::

You can edit and delete every post whose group you would be allowed to choose yourself – so also
those of colleagues in your area. The list shows who wrote each post.

## Common questions

### Why can't I choose “All volunteers of this edition”?

That needs your role to have the permission for the whole edition. With a role for an area, you write
to your area and to individual shifts in it.

### How do I reach just one shift?

Open the shift and tap _Message everyone_. That is the only way to choose _Everyone on this shift_.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>What happens to a post when its shift or area is deleted?</summary>

It then reaches nobody – better nobody than everyone. After that, only an admin can change it.

</details>
