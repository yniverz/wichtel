You lead an area or a shift and have a role with permissions in the admin area (_Admin_). **What you
see there depends on your role** – on its permissions and on which areas it applies to. If something
you need is missing, ask your festival's admins: only they (or leads allowed to assign roles) can
change roles.

### How do I get to the admin area?

In the volunteer view, the menu has _Admin_; the home page also has _Go to admin_. Both only appear if
you have a role in the current edition.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/admin-area/)

### Why are menu items missing that others have?

Each menu item only appears if your role has the matching permission. Some items even need the
permission for the whole edition, not just for your area.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/admin-area/)

### Why do I only see the shifts of my area?

Your role applies to certain areas and their sub-areas. Everywhere in the admin area you only see
what lies within those areas.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/admin-area/)

### Where can I see where people are still missing?

On the _Overview_: staffing in percent, a coloured table by area and day, and the next shifts with
free places.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/dashboard/)

### How do I create a shift?

_Shifts_ → _New shift_, enter area, title, date and time, set the positions, _Create_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/)

### How do I create many identical shifts at once?

_Shifts_ → _Create series_: choose the date range, weekdays and time slots. The page shows
beforehand how many shifts will be created.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/series/)

### How do I cancel a shift?

Open the shift → at the very bottom _Delete shift_. Everyone signed up gets an e-mail saying the
shift will not take place. This cannot be undone.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/)

### What is a position?

A type of place within a shift, e.g. “Volunteer” and “Shift lead”, each with its own capacity, its
own booking mode and its own requirements.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/positions/)

### How do I let only selected people onto a position?

Set the position to _on request_ – then you confirm every booking yourself. Or make a qualification
_required_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/positions/)

### How do I sign someone up myself?

Open the shift → at the position _+ Add person_ → search for the name → _Add_. The person gets an
e-mail.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)

### How do I record who was there?

On the shift's page you set _present_ or _no-show_ for each person – from the day of the shift. Only
_present_ gives the person their points.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)

### Where do I approve requests and handovers?

Under _Requests_. The number next to the menu item shows how much is open.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/requests/)

### People are missing at short notice – what can I do?

Open the shift → at the position _Urgent call …_. Everyone who fits and is free gets an e-mail with a
direct link, with bonus points if you like.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/urgent/)

### Where do I find a person's phone number?

In the shift's staffing list, if your role has the permission _View volunteer contact details_ for
the area.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/people/)

### How do I review qualification proofs?

_Qualifications_ → _To review_: view the proof, optionally write a note, _Approve_ or _Decline_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/qualifications/)

### How do I write to everyone in my area?

_News_ → _New post_ → _For_: “Everyone with shifts in an area”. With _Also send by e-mail_ it also
goes out as an e-mail.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/news/)

### How do I check someone in at the volunteer desk?

Scan the person's QR code with your phone camera or search for them under _Desk & check-in_, then
_Check in_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/desk/)

### How do I print a shift plan or a list to tick off?

_Shifts_ → _Print shift plan_, or on a shift's page _Print crew list_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/print/)

### Can I use Claude for planning?

Yes, if your role has the permission _Connect an AI assistant_. The instructions are in your profile
under _Connect Claude_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/leads/ai/)
