title: Reviewing qualifications

:::tip[At a glance]

- **Review:** _Admin_ → _Qualifications_ → _To review_ → view the proof → optionally _Note to the
  person (optional)_ → _Approve_ or _Decline_.
- **You can only open documents** with the permission _View uploaded documents_.
- **Grant directly:** person page → _Qualifications_ → choose the qualification → _Grant_.
- Depending on the setting, a document is **deleted after the review**.

:::

Qualifications are proofs such as a hygiene training or a driving licence. Positions can mark them
as _required_ (see [Positions](/wichtel/en/leads/positions/)). Volunteers provide them in the app –
by ticking a box, by uploading a document or both, depending on how the admins set up the
qualification.

## Step by step: reviewing proofs

1. Open _Qualifications_. Under _To review_ you find all open proofs, oldest first.
2. Each entry shows: the person (link to the person page if you may open _People_), the
   qualification, the date, a note from
   the person in quotation marks, and the proof:
   - _View proof: {file name}_ – opens the document in a new tab (only with the permission _View
     uploaded documents_; otherwise only the file name is shown),
   - or _confirmed only, no document_.
3. If needed, write a _Note to the person (optional)_, e.g. “Please upload the back as well”. The
   person sees it if you decline.
4. Tap _Approve_ or _Decline_.

The person sees the result (_confirmed_ or _declined_, with your note when declined) in the app under
_Qualifications_. No e-mail is sent for this. If the qualification is only valid for a while, the
period starts when you approve, and the person sees “valid until …”.

Under _Recently decided_ you see the latest decisions.

## Granting a qualification directly

If you already know the proof (e.g. because you gave the training yourself), no request is needed:

1. Open the person page (_People_ → tap the person).
2. In the _Qualifications_ section, choose the qualification and tap _Grant_.

_Revoke_ takes a confirmed qualification away again; it then counts as _declined_. For the person
page you need access to _People_ (see [Finding people](/wichtel/en/leads/people/)).

## What happens to the documents

For every qualification the admins set under _Documents_:

- _keep_ – the document stays stored.
- _delete after review_ – the document is deleted as soon as you approve or decline. After that,
  nobody can open it any more; the result stays.

If a person submits a new proof, it replaces the old one; the old document is deleted.

:::note
Proofs can only be seen by the person themselves and by whoever has the permission _View uploaded
documents_. This permission only works through roles in the current edition.
:::

## Common questions

### Why do I see proofs from people who don't help in my area at all?

Qualifications apply to all areas and editions. Whoever has _Review and grant qualifications_
therefore sees all open proofs, regardless of which area their role applies to.

### Can I create new qualifications?

No, the admins do that (see [Setting up qualifications](/wichtel/en/organizers/qualifications/)).

### The person has a qualification but still cannot sign up

Check on their person page that the qualification is _confirmed_ and not expired. A proof that is
_under review_ is not enough yet.

## Special cases

<summary>Is it recorded who reviewed what?</summary>

Yes. Approving, declining, granting and revoking are recorded in the audit log, your note as the
reason.
