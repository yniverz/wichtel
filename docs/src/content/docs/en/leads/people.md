:::tip[At a glance]

- **Search:** _Admin_ → _People_ → enter name or email → _Search_.
- **Phone number, quickly:** in the shift's staffing list – if your role has _View volunteer contact
  details_ for the area.
- **Email addresses and profile details** are only visible with this permission for the whole
  edition.
- The _People_ menu item only exists with _View volunteer contact details_ or _Assign roles (only with
  own permissions)_.

:::

## Step by step

1. Open _People_. The list shows all accounts of this Wichtel installation – not just this year's
   volunteers –, sorted by last name, 50 per page.
2. Enter part of the name or email address and tap _Search_.
3. Tap a person to open their page.

The list shows hints such as _Admin_ or _unconfirmed_ (the person has not confirmed their email
address yet).

## What the person page shows

| Section                | What                                                                                                                             | Visible if …                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Header                 | Name, _Registered on …_, hints _Admin_ / _unconfirmed_                                                                           | always                                                                                      |
| _Shifts_               | The person's shifts in the selected edition with their status (e.g. _present_, _no-show_, _Requested_, _Waiting list_), as links | only shifts in areas for which you have shift permissions                                   |
| _Roles in edition …_   | Their roles; with _Assign role_ if you may assign roles                                                                          | always (assigning only with _Assign roles (only with own permissions)_)                     |
| _Points_ → _Open desk_ | Jump to the desk for this person                                                                                                 | you have access to _Desk & check-in_                                                        |
| _Contact_              | Email address and phone number                                                                                                   | _View volunteer contact details_ for the whole edition                                      |
| _Details_              | Answers to the festival's profile fields                                                                                         | as for _Contact_                                                                            |
| _Qualifications_       | Their qualifications; grant and revoke                                                                                           | _Review and grant qualifications_ (see [Qualifications](/wichtel/en/leads/qualifications/)) |

Only admins can change accounts, create password links and delete accounts (see
[Managing people](/wichtel/en/organizers/people/)).

## Which contact details you see where

| Where                                      | What                         | Permission needed                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| A shift's staffing list, printed crew list | Phone number                 | _View volunteer contact details_ in the shift's area     |
| People list, person page                   | Email, phone number, details | _View volunteer contact details_ for the whole edition   |
| Search when adding someone to a shift      | Email                        | _View volunteer contact details_ for the whole edition   |
| _Desk & check-in_                          | Email, phone number          | _View volunteer contact details_ anywhere in the edition |

Profile details the admins have explicitly released for leads (e.g. T-shirt size) appear in the
staffing list even without this permission.

## Common questions

### I lead an area and still don't see any email addresses

Your permission applies to your area. There you see the phone numbers in the staffing lists. Email
and details on the person page need the permission for the whole edition. Ask the admins if you need
them.

### I click a name and get “You do not have permission to do this.”

The person page belongs to the _People_ menu item. Without _View volunteer contact details_ or
_Assign roles (only with own permissions)_ you cannot open it, even if names elsewhere appear as
links.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>Why did the contact details disappear when I switched the edition?</summary>

The permission _View volunteer contact details_ only works through roles in the current edition. If
you are working on another edition at the top, contact details stay hidden.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Deleted accounts</summary>

Deleted accounts appear neither in the list nor in the search. In old shifts they remain without a
name for the statistics.

</details>
