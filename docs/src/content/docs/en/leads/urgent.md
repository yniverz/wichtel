title: Urgent call
description: Find people at short notice – who gets the e-mail, how bonus points work and how to end the call.

:::tip[At a glance]

- **Start:** open the shift → at the position _Urgent call …_ → optionally _Bonus points_ and _Short
  message (optional)_ → _Send call_.
- **Who gets the e-mail:** everyone with a confirmed account who fits and is free at that time.
- **Bonus points** go to whoever signs up while the call is running.
- **End:** at the position _End call_.
- At most once per hour per position.

:::

## Step by step

1. Open the shift (e.g. from _Understaffed next_ on the [overview](/wichtel/en/leads/dashboard/)).
2. At the position that is short of people, tap _Urgent call …_. The link only appears while the
   shift has not started and places are free.
3. Optionally:
   - _Bonus points_ (0 to 100) – “For everyone who signs up now. 0 = no bonus.”
   - _Short message (optional)_ – up to 300 characters, included in the e-mail.
4. Tap _Send call_. You see “Call sent to {number} people.”
5. The position now shows “Urgent since {time}” (with “+{bonus}” if you gave bonus points).

## Who gets the e-mail

All accounts with a confirmed email address that

- are not on this shift yet (not on the waiting list either),
- have no other shift at that time (including the required break),
- have all qualifications the position marks as _required_,
- for an internal shift, have a role that covers the area.

The e-mail names the shift, the position, the number of free places, time, place, your message and
the bonus, and contains a direct link to the shift. It goes out in each person's language.

## What volunteers see

While the call is running and places are free, the position is marked _Urgent_ in the shift list,
with “+{bonus} bonus points”. On their home page the shift appears under _Urgently needed_. Everyone
can sign up for a position with a running call, even if their booking wave is not open yet.

## Bonus points

Whoever signs up for the position while the call is running gets the bonus points on top of the
normal points. Like all shift points, they are only credited once the person is ticked off as
_present_ (see [Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)).

## Ending the call

Tap _End call_ at the position. From then on nobody gets the bonus points any more; people who have
already signed up keep theirs.

The call does not end by itself when the position is full. Volunteers no longer see it then, but if a
place becomes free again, the bonus still applies. So end it when you are done.

## Common questions

### “A call for this position just went out. Please wait an hour.”

So that nobody gets flooded with e-mails, at most one call per hour goes out per position.

### Can I change the bonus afterwards?

After an hour you can call again and set a new bonus. That sends the e-mail again, though.

### Who may make an urgent call?

Anyone who may _Add and remove volunteers, approve requests_ in the shift's area.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>Do people get the e-mail who are not taking part this year yet?</summary>

Yes. The search covers all accounts with a confirmed address, not just those who already have a shift
in this edition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the call recorded in the audit log?</summary>

Yes, with the bonus and the number of recipients. Ending it is recorded too.

</details>
