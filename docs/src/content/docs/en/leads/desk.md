:::tip[At a glance]

- **Open a person:** scan their QR code with your phone camera – or _Desk & check-in_ → search for
  the name.
- **Check in:** under _Shifts today_ → _Check in_. This works from 0:00 on the day of the shift.
- **Goodies:** under _To pick up_ → _Hand out_.
- **Mandatory goodies** such as the free ticket appear under _To pick up_ by themselves as soon as the
  points are enough.

:::

You see the _Desk & check-in_ menu item if your role has _Hand out goodies_ or _Confirm attendance
(check-in)_, or _Adjust points manually_ for the whole edition. What you can do on a person's page
depends on which of these permissions you have.

## Opening a person

### With the QR code

Every volunteer has a personal QR code in the app under _Points & goodies_.

1. Open your phone's camera app and point it at the code.
2. Open the link the camera recognises. You need to be logged in to Wichtel on the phone.
3. You land directly on the person's desk page.

This works if your role in the current edition has _Hand out goodies_ or _Confirm attendance
(check-in)_. Otherwise Wichtel reports “This code is unknown.”

### Searching by hand

1. Open _Desk & check-in_.
2. Type at least two letters of the name under _Find person_ and tap _Search_. You can only search by
   email address with _View volunteer contact details_.
3. Tap the person.

Only people taking part in this edition are found (with a shift, a goodie or points), at most 20
results.

## A person's page

At the top are the name and _confirmed points_ (plus “+{number} pending from booked shifts”), with
the contact permission also the phone number.

### Shifts today

All of the person's shifts that start today or are running right now.

- _Check in_ sets the attendance to _present_. The shift's points are credited immediately.
- If the person is already _checked in_, _Undo_ sets it back to _open_ (the points are deducted
  again).

You can only check in shifts in areas for which you have _Confirm attendance (check-in)_, and from
0:00 on the day of the shift – so also in the morning before an evening shift. You set _no-show_ on
the shift's page (see [Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)).

### To pick up

Goodies the person has chosen or that were redeemed automatically, with the variant (e.g. size) and
the details the goodie needs.

- _Hand out_ – you have handed it over.
- _Undo_ – the choice is cancelled and the person gets the points back.
- If it shows _refund pending_, the person chose “I already have one” (e.g. they already have a
  ticket). Then tap _Mark refunded_ once the refund is done.

### Hand out directly

Goodies the person has not chosen beforehand can be handed out here right away – as far as they are
eligible and the points are enough.

### Correct points

Only with _Adjust points manually_ for the whole edition: enter _Points (+/−)_ and a _Reason_, then
_Book_. The reason appears in the person's points history and in the audit log.

### Points history

The latest entries on the points account: shifts, goodies and corrections.

## Mandatory goodies

Some goodies are mandatory goodies, e.g. the free ticket. As soon as a person has enough confirmed
points through check-in, the mandatory goodie is redeemed automatically and immediately appears under
_To pick up_ – you can hand it out right after checking them in. If there are several, they are
redeemed in the set order.

## Common questions

### The “Check in” button is missing

Either the shift is in an area for which you have no check-in permission, or it does not start
today.

### The person cannot be found

If they have no shift, no goodie and no points in this edition yet, they do not appear here. Add them
to a shift first (see [Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)).

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>The mandatory goodie does not appear after a points correction</summary>

Mandatory goodies are redeemed after attendance is ticked off, not after a manual points correction.
If the points are enough, you can hand out the goodie under _Hand out directly_.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it recorded who handed out what?</summary>

Yes. Check-ins, hand-outs, cancellations, refunds and points corrections are recorded in the audit
log.

</details>
