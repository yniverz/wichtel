---
title: Profile fields
description: Add extra questions for volunteers – when they are asked, whether they are required and who sees the answers.
---

:::tip[At a glance]

- **Create:** _Admin → People → Profile fields_ → _New field_ (admins only).
- **When to ask:** _at sign-up_, _only in the profile_ or _when choosing certain goodies_.
- **Ask as late as possible** – that keeps sign-up short.
- Wichtel always asks for name, e-mail and phone itself; you need no fields for those.
- Admins and people with the permission _View volunteer contact details_ see the answers – leads in
  shift rosters only if you allow it.

:::

## Step by step

1. Open _Profile fields_ and expand _+ New field_.
2. Enter the _Question (German)_ and _Question (English)_, e.g. “T-shirt size”.
3. Optional: a _Hint_ in both languages shown below the question, e.g. “We order based on your
   answers.”
4. Choose the _Type_ (see below). For _Choice_ and _Multiple choice_, enter the _Options_
   comma-separated, e.g. `S, M, L, XL`.
5. Under _Ask_, choose when the question is asked. For _when choosing certain goodies_, tick the
   goodies.
6. Tick _Required_ if an answer is needed.
7. Tick _Leads see the value in shift rosters_ if leads need the value for their shifts.
8. Leave _Active_ ticked and tap _Create_.

## Field types

| _Type_            | Answer                                  |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| _Text_            | one line, up to 200 characters          |
| _Long text_       | several lines, up to 2000 characters    |
| _Number_          | a number (comma or point)               |
| _Date_            | a date                                  |
| _Choice_          | exactly one of the options              |
| _Multiple choice_ | one or more of the options              |
| _Yes/No_          | a checkbox                              |

A _Yes/No_ field marked _Required_ must be ticked – good for agreements like “I have read the house
rules”.

## When the question is asked

| _Ask_                             | Where volunteers answer                                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _at sign-up_                      | in the sign-up form; can be changed later in the profile                                       |
| _only in the profile_             | in the profile                                                                                 |
| _when choosing certain goodies_   | when someone chooses one of the selected goodies (“For this we still need: …”)                 |

If someone is missing an answer to a required sign-up or profile field (e.g. because you added the
field later), their home page says “Please complete your details in your profile.” If a goodie field
is required, the goodie cannot be chosen without an answer.

## Who sees the answers

- **The person themselves** in their profile (sign-up and profile fields) and in their data export.
- **Admins and everyone with the permission _View volunteer contact details_ for the whole edition**
  on the person's page under _Details_.
- **Leads** in a shift's roster – only for fields with _Leads see the value in shift rosters_. Typical:
  allergies for the catering team.
- **The desk**: answers to goodie fields are shown at the [desk](/wichtel/en/leads/desk/) next to the
  goodie to be picked up, e.g. the chosen size.

## Privacy

Every question is personal data that you store and must protect.

- **Only ask what you really need** – and only when you need it. The T-shirt size belongs to the goodie
  choice, not to sign-up.
- **Be careful with health data** such as allergies or intolerances: it is specially protected. Make
  such fields optional and show them only to the leads who need them.
- **Include profile fields in the [privacy policy](/wichtel/en/organizers/privacy/).** The template
  there takes the fields you created into account.

## Common questions

### Can I delete a field?

No, only deactivate it: untick _Active_. Wichtel then no longer asks for it.

### Do profile fields start over for every edition?

No. Profile fields and answers belong to the whole installation. Anyone who answered last year
doesn't have to answer again.
