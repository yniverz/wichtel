:::tip[At a glance]

- **Where:** _Admin → System → Settings_, first card _Appearance_.
- On the right, the _Preview_ shows every change immediately; it is applied only with _Save_.
- Each colour shows how readable text on it is. Aim for at least **4.5:1**.
- Light or dark mode is chosen by the person's device; your colours apply in both.

:::

## Step by step

1. Enter the _Festival name_. It appears everywhere in the app, in the browser tab and in e-mails.
2. Optional: _Tagline (German)_ and _Tagline (English)_, e.g. “Volunteering at the summer festival”.
   If the English version is missing, Wichtel shows the German one.
3. Choose the _Primary color_ and _Accent color_ – with the colour picker or as a code like `#c03a1c`.
4. Upload a _Logo_, _Background image_ and _Browser icon (favicon)_ if you like.
5. Check the _Preview_ and the contrast notes below the colours.
6. Tap _Save_ at the bottom.

## Where things appear

| Setting                  | Where                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _Festival name_          | header, start page, browser tab, e-mails                                                        |
| _Tagline_                | start page below the name; on large screens next to login and sign-up                           |
| _Primary color_          | buttons such as _Sign up_, links, highlights; also the colour of the browser bar on some phones |
| _Accent color_           | small highlights, e.g. points badges                                                            |
| _Logo_                   | header, left of the name. Without a logo, the first letter appears there in the primary colour. |
| _Background image_       | as a wide image on the start page; on large screens next to login and sign-up                   |
| _Browser icon (favicon)_ | in the browser tab and in bookmarks. Without your own icon, Wichtel uses its own.               |

## Colours and contrast

On every colour, Wichtel automatically writes in black or white – whichever is easier to read. Below
each colour you see the result:

- “Contrast 5.4:1 – easy to read.” – all good.
- “Contrast 3.2:1 – text on this color is hard to read. At least 4.5:1 is recommended.” – you can
  still save, but buttons will then be hard to read for many people, especially outdoors in the sun.
  Choose the colour a bit darker or lighter.

Medium tones (light red, orange, mid green) often score poorly. The default colours `#c03a1c` (primary)
and `#f4c430` (accent) meet the recommendation.

## Images

- PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG and ICO are allowed, max. 5 MB each.
- **Logo**: it is shown about 32 pixels high. Best is a wide logo with a transparent background that
  works on light and dark backgrounds.
- **Background image**: a landscape photo. On the login page, Wichtel puts a dark gradient over it and
  writes the name in white – a calm motif without much text works best.
- **Browser icon**: square, e.g. 512 × 512 pixels as PNG, or as SVG.
- A new image replaces the old one. With _Remove image_ and _Save_ you delete one.

## Light and dark mode

Wichtel follows the device setting: if dark mode is on there, the app appears dark. Your primary colour
stays the same on buttons; for links and coloured text, Wichtel automatically lightens it in dark mode
so it stays readable on the dark background. So check your logo in dark mode once, too.

## Language

The _Default language_ is on the same page in the card _General_. Wichtel uses it for visitors whose
browser prefers neither German nor English. Logged-in people see the language from their profile, and
everyone can switch at the top. See [Settings](/wichtel/en/organizers/settings/).
