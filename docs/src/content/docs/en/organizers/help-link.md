:::tip[At a glance]

- **Every page** of the app has a help link that opens the matching page of this help in the person's
  language.
- **Own FAQ:** _Admin → System → Settings_ → card _Help_ → _Own help page_ → enter the address →
  _Save_.
- Help links for volunteers then go to your FAQ; the admin area keeps linking to this help.
- Empty field = Wichtel help.

:::

## Where the help links are

| Where                                                                            | Link                       | Without an own FAQ, leads to                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Admin area                                                                       | “?” next to the page title | the matching page of this help                  |
| Volunteer area                                                                   | “?” in the header          | the matching page of this help                  |
| Log in, sign up, forgot password                                                 | “?” in the header          | the matching page of this help                  |
| Footer in the volunteer area, on login and sign-up and on the other public pages | _Help_                     | the section [I volunteer](/wichtel/en/helpers/) |

The links open in a new tab and in the language the person is currently using the app in.

## Linking your own FAQ

Many questions from volunteers cannot be answered by this help because it doesn't know your festival:
When is the goodie counter open? Where do I park? What should I bring? If you have your own FAQ for
that, send volunteers there.

1. Open _Admin → System → Settings_.
2. In the card _Help_, enter the full address of your FAQ under _Own help page_, starting with
   `https://`, e.g. `https://festival.example/faq`.
3. Tap _Save_ at the bottom.

After that, these links go to your FAQ:

- the “?” in the header of the volunteer area and of the login and sign-up pages,
- _Help_ in the footer – in the volunteer area, on the login and sign-up pages and on the other public
  pages.

**The admin area keeps linking to this Wichtel help**, because your FAQ won't explain how to plan
shifts. To return to the Wichtel help, empty the field and save.

Your FAQ usually exists in one language only. All volunteers then land there, whatever language they
have set. If you have a bilingual page, link the start page where people choose the language.

## What a good festival FAQ contains

- **Arrival**: address, directions, parking, where the volunteer desk is and when it is open.
- **Goodies**: pickup times and place, what there is, how the free ticket works.
- **What to bring**: clothing, shoes, water bottle, ID, proofs for qualifications.
- **On site**: food and drinks for volunteers, sleeping place or camping, showers.
- **Contact**: whom to call or write to with problems – also shortly before or during a shift.
- **Cancelling**: what to do when you get ill, and until when you can cancel yourself.

Questions about the app itself (signing up, swapping, groups, points) are answered by this help. Just
link to it from your FAQ:

- German: `https://yniverz.github.io/wichtel/helpers/`
- English: `https://yniverz.github.io/wichtel/en/helpers/`

:::tip
Short, current notes such as changed pickup times are better placed in [news](/wichtel/en/leads/news/)
– volunteers see them right in the app.
:::

## Common questions

### Can I link my own help for the admin area too?

No. The admin area always links to this Wichtel help.

### Why doesn't my address work?

Enter the full address, i.e. with `https://` at the beginning. An address without it (e.g. just
`festival.example/faq`) is rejected.
