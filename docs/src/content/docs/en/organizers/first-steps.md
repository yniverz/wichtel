:::tip[At a glance]

- Open the **setup link** from the server log → festival name, your admin account and the first
  edition.
- Then from top to bottom: settings, legal pages, areas, roles, places, rules, shifts.
- **Finally:** send a test e-mail and open registration.
- Everything can be changed later – the order just saves detours.

:::

## Step by step

1. **Set up.** Open the setup link the server writes to its log on first start (see
   [Install](/wichtel/en/operators/install/)). On the page _Welcome to Wichtel_ you enter:
   - _Your festival_: the _Festival name_,
   - _Your admin account_: name, e-mail address, mobile number and password,
   - _First edition_: name and period including setup and teardown.

   After _Finish setup_ you are signed in and land in the _Admin_ area. The link stops working
   afterwards. The first edition automatically becomes the current one, and five roles already exist
   as templates. [More on editions](/wichtel/en/organizers/editions/)

2. **Settings and appearance.** Under _Settings_, check _General_ (time zone, default language,
   contact email) and design logo and colours under _Appearance_. The language you used for setup is
   the default language at first.
   [Settings](/wichtel/en/organizers/settings/) · [Appearance](/wichtel/en/organizers/appearance/)

3. **Legal notice and privacy.** Under _Privacy & legal notice_, enter the details for the legal
   notice and insert the template for the privacy policy. Do this before the first volunteers sign
   up. [Privacy & legal notice](/wichtel/en/organizers/privacy/)

4. **Areas.** Create the structure of your festival, e.g. _Setup_, _Bar_, _Awareness_ with
   sub-areas. [Areas](/wichtel/en/organizers/areas/)

5. **Roles and leads.** Review the role templates under _Roles_ and assign them to your leads under
   _People_ – they need an account for that. [Roles and permissions](/wichtel/en/organizers/roles/)

6. **Places.** Under _Places & site plan_, upload your site plan, set places with a pin and choose
   the place of the _Volunteer desk_. [Places & site plan](/wichtel/en/organizers/places/)

7. **Booking rules and points.** Cancellation deadline, minimum break, waiting list and swaps under
   _Settings_ → _Booking_ and _Swaps & groups_; the base point rule and bonuses under _Settings_ →
   _Points_. [Booking rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/booking-rules/) ·
   [Point rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/points/)

8. **Goodies.** Create the rewards, e.g. a free ticket as a mandatory goodie.
   [Goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

9. **Profile fields.** Think about what you need to know from volunteers – and ask as late as
   possible. [Profile fields](/wichtel/en/organizers/fields/)

10. **Qualifications.** Create proofs such as a food hygiene training before shifts need them as a
    requirement. [Qualifications](/wichtel/en/organizers/qualifications/)

11. **Booking waves.** Should the crew book first? Then plan the waves before you open
    registration. Without waves, booking is open to everyone right away.
    [Booking waves](/wichtel/en/organizers/waves/)

12. **Shifts.** Now you or the leads plan the shifts – one by one or as a series.
    [Creating shifts](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/) · [Series](/wichtel/en/leads/series/)

13. **Test e-mail.** Under _Delivery_ → _Send a test e-mail to me_ you check that e-mails go out.
    [E-mails](/wichtel/en/organizers/mail/)

14. **Open registration.** Under _Settings_ → _General_ you find _Registration open for new
    volunteers_. Then share the link to your Wichtel site.
    [Settings](/wichtel/en/organizers/settings/)

:::caution[Registration is open out of the box]

Anyone who knows the address can sign up right away and – without booking waves – book shifts. If
you want to set up in peace, untick _Registration open for new volunteers_ right at the start and
tick it again in step 14.

:::

## Common questions

### Where do I find the setup link?

In the server log, on first start. Whoever runs Wichtel finds it there; how is described in
[Install](/wichtel/en/operators/install/). Once setup is complete, the link forwards to the sign-in.

### The overview shows "Getting started" – is that enough?

The admin overview reminds you of three things: logo and colours, areas and role assignments. This
page is the complete list.

### Do I need a mail server?

It works without one, but then Wichtel sends no e-mails, addresses are not confirmed, and nobody can
reset their password themselves. The admin overview points this out. You then create reset links on
the person's page.
[Managing people](/wichtel/en/organizers/people/) · [Setting up e-mail](/wichtel/en/operators/email/)
