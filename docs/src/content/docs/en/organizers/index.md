This part of the help is for **admins**: the people who set up the whole festival in Wichtel –
editions, areas, roles, rules for volunteers, data protection. You find all of it in the _Admin_
area (in the menu of the volunteer view).

:::tip[At a glance]

- **Just installed?** Work through the [checklist for getting started](/wichtel/en/organizers/first-steps/).
- **Planning and staffing shifts** is explained in [I lead an area](/wichtel/en/leads/).
- **Installation and server** are covered in [I run the server](/wichtel/en/operators/).

:::

## Structure

### How do I set up next year's festival?

Under _Editions_ → _Copy edition_. Areas are always copied; places, shifts, goodies, role
assignments and booking waves as you choose – all times moved by whole days. Bookings and points
never come along.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/editions/)

### Which year do volunteers see?

Always the edition with the _Current_ badge. You change it under _Editions_ with _Make current
edition_. The _Edition_ switch at the top of the admin area only changes your own view.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/editions/)

### How do I build the structure of the festival?

With _Areas_: nested to any depth, e.g. working group → area → team. Deadlines, points and roles on
an area also apply to all its sub-areas.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/areas/)

### How do I make someone an area lead?

_People_ → open the person → under _Roles in edition …_ choose the role _Area lead_ and under
_Applies to_ the area → _Assign role_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/roles/)

### Someone cannot see a menu item – why?

A permission is missing from their role, or the role only applies to one area while the page needs
the permission for the whole edition (e.g. _Goodies_, _Booking waves_, _Places & site plan_). Roles
also always apply to one edition only.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/roles/)

### How do I show volunteers where things are?

Under _Places & site plan_, upload a plan and create places with a pin. Shifts and goodies then
refer to these places.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/places/)

## Rules for volunteers

### Until when can volunteers cancel?

The default is 48 hours before the start, set under _Settings_ → _Booking_. Areas and single shifts
can have their own deadline.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/booking-rules/)

### How do I let the crew book first?

With _Booking waves_: each wave opens booking at a time for a group, e.g. the crew first, then
everyone. Without waves, booking is always open to everyone.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/waves/)

### How many points does a shift give?

Out of the box, 1 point per shift. The base rule is under _Settings_ → _Points_; areas and positions
can override it.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/points/)

### How do I set up goodies, e.g. the free ticket?

Under _Goodies_ → _New goodie_. A mandatory goodie is redeemed automatically as soon as someone has
enough points.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### How do I require a training for a shift?

Create the qualification under _Qualifications_ and set it to _required_ or _nice to have_ on the
shift's position.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/qualifications/)

### How do I ask for, say, the T-shirt size?

With your own field under _Profile fields_. You decide when to ask: at sign-up, only in the profile,
or when choosing certain goodies.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/fields/)

## People and communication

### A confirmation e-mail does not arrive – what now?

Open the person under _People_ and tap _Mark as confirmed_ – but only if you are sure the address
belongs to them. For typos, correct the address under _Edit details_.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/people/)

### Someone forgot their password and gets no e-mail.

On the person's page, under _Reset password_, create a link and hand it over in person. It is valid
for one hour and works once.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/people/)

### Do our e-mails arrive at all?

Under _Delivery_ → _Send a test e-mail to me_ you check sending. There you also see failed e-mails
and the history.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/mail/)

### Can volunteers see our own FAQ instead of this help?

Yes: _Settings_ → _Help_ → _Own help page_. The admin area keeps linking to this help.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/help-link/)

## System

### Where do I change logo, colours and name?

Under _Settings_ → _Appearance_, with a preview and a contrast check.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/appearance/)

### Where do I maintain the legal notice and privacy policy?

Under _Privacy & legal notice_. The legal notice is built from your details; for the privacy policy
there is a template with parts in [square brackets] to complete.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/privacy/)

### Someone asks by letter what we store about them.

Open the person and download the _data export_ under _Data protection_. There you can also delete the
account on request.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/privacy/)

### Who changed this?

The _Audit log_ shows it: every admin action with person and time, changes by AI assistants with a
marker.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/audit/)

### How do I enable AI assistants such as Claude?

Give suitable roles the permission _Connect an AI assistant_ and decide under _Settings_ → _AI
assistants (MCP)_ what assistants may change.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/ai/)

### How do I open or close registration?

_Settings_ → _General_ → _Registration open for new volunteers_. It is open out of the box.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/organizers/settings/)
