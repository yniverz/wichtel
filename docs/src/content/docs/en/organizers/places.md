:::tip[At a glance]

- **Site plan:** _Places & site plan_ → _Upload plan_ (a picture of your grounds).
- **Create a place:** _+ New place_ → name, directions, pin on the plan and/or coordinates.
- **Use it:** in every shift under _Place (with pin)_ and _Meeting point (with pin)_, for goodies as
  _Pickup place_.
- **Volunteer desk:** choose the place – volunteers see it on their home page.
- Places and plan belong to one edition.

:::

## Step by step

### Uploading the site plan

1. Open _Places & site plan_.
2. Under _Site plan_, choose a picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, at most 5 MB) and tap _Upload plan_.

A new plan replaces the old one. _Remove plan_ deletes it.

### Creating a place

1. Tap _+ New place_.
2. Enter the _Name_ (German, ideally also English), e.g. "Stage 2" or "Volunteer tent".
3. Optional: _Directions_, e.g. "Behind the drinks stand on the left", and an _Address_.
4. **Pin on the site plan:** click the plan. _Remove pin_ takes it away again.
5. **Coordinates** for the map: _Load map to place the pin_ and click the map – or paste e.g.
   "50.1109, 8.6821" or a Google Maps link.
6. Tap _Create_.

A place does not need both. On the grounds, the pin on the plan is often enough; for car parks or a
station, coordinates or an address are more practical.

### Setting the volunteer desk

Under _Volunteer desk_, choose the place and save. Volunteers then see it on their home page under
"How to find the volunteer desk". If a goodie has no pickup place of its own, this place applies.

### Using places in shifts

When creating or editing a shift, choose one of your places under _Place (with pin)_ and _Meeting
point (with pin)_. The free-text fields _Location_ and _Meeting point_ add details, e.g. "behind the
tent". The selection appears as soon as there is at least one place.
[Creating shifts](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/)

### Using places for goodies

For the goodie, choose a place under _Pickup place_ and add e.g. the times under _Pickup details_.
[Goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

## Common questions

### What do volunteers see?

For a shift or a goodie: the place with directions, the pin on the site plan and links under "Open
in:" to map apps (Google Maps, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap). These links exist if the place has
coordinates or an address.

### Is a map loaded from an outside service?

Only when someone taps _Load map_; before that there is a note that their IP address goes to the map
service. The site plan and the links work without an outside service. You choose the map server
under _Settings_ → _Maps_. [Settings](/wichtel/en/organizers/settings/)

### Who may manage places?

Admins and everyone with _Create and edit shifts_ for the _Whole edition_.
[Roles and permissions](/wichtel/en/organizers/roles/)

### What happens if I delete a place?

Shifts keep their free-text details but lose the pin. Goodies lose their pickup place. If the place
was the volunteer desk, none is set afterwards.

## Background

Places and site plan belong to the edition. When [copying an edition](/wichtel/en/organizers/editions/)
you take them along with _Places, site plan and volunteer desk_. Every change is recorded in the
[audit log](/wichtel/en/organizers/audit/).
