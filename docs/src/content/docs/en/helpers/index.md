These are the questions volunteers ask most often. Each answer is short – "Details" takes you to
the full guide.

:::note[Questions about your festival]
Times, getting there, goodie pickup, food and everything else that only applies to your festival
cannot be answered by this help. The organisers answer those: check _News_ in the app or write to
them via the _Contact_ link at the very bottom of the app. The help link in the app may also point
to your festival's own FAQ instead of this site.
:::

## Account

### How do I sign up?

Tap _Sign up as a volunteer_, fill in your name, email address, mobile number and a password with
at least 10 characters, then open the link in the confirmation email.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/)

### The confirmation email does not arrive.

Check your spam folder. Log in and tap _Resend email_. If that does not help, the organisers can
mark your address as confirmed by hand.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/#no-confirmation-email)

### I forgot my password.

Tap _Forgot your password?_ on the login page. You get a link that is valid for one hour.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/#forgot-password)

## Shifts

### How do I sign up for a shift?

_Shifts_ → pick a day → tap a shift → tap _Sign up_ next to the right position. You get a
confirmation by email. [→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/)

### Why can't I sign up?

Usually booking is not open for you yet, the shift overlaps with another one, or a qualification
is missing. What the app shows tells you the reason.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/cannot-book/)

### What does "Requested" mean?

For some positions a lead decides who joins. You tapped _Request_, and the lead confirms or
declines – either way you get an email.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/#direct-or-on-request)

### The shift is full – now what?

Tap _Join waiting list_. If a place becomes free, you move up automatically and get an email.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/#waiting-list)

### How do I cancel a shift?

Tap the shift → _Cancel_. This works until the deadline shown (usually 48 hours before the start).
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)

### The cancellation deadline has passed.

Pass the shift on with _Give away …_ – on the shift market or directly to one person. You stay
signed up until someone takes over.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/#after-the-deadline-give-the-shift-away)

### How do I volunteer together with friends?

Start a group under _Profile_ → _Group_ and send the invite link around. Then you see each other's
shifts and can sign up together. [→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/group/)

### How do I get my shifts into my phone calendar?

_Profile_ → _Calendar feed_ → _Subscribe in calendar_. New and changed shifts then arrive by
themselves. [→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/calendar/)

## Points and goodies

### When do I get my points?

As soon as it is confirmed that you were at the shift – usually at check-in at the volunteer desk
or by the shift lead. Until then they show as "pending".
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/)

### How do I get a goodie?

_Goodies_ → _Choose_ on the goodie you want → pick it up at the counter and show your code.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/#choosing-goodies)

### A shift requires a qualification.

Tap _Provide now_ in the shift or go to _Profile_ → _Manage qualifications_. Once it has been
reviewed, you can sign up. [→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/qualifications/)

## On site

### Where do I find my QR code?

Under _Goodies_ → _Your code_. Show it at the volunteer desk or the goodie counter.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/)

### How do I find the meeting point?

Tap the shift and then the place name next to _Meeting point_. You see the site plan and links to
Google Maps or Apple Maps. [→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/#finding-places)

### Where do I see news from the organisers?

Under _News_. The number on the menu item shows how many posts are new.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/news/)

## Emails and data

### I get no emails.

Check your spam folder and look in your _Profile_ to see which address you are signed in with. If
it is wrong, only the organisers can change it.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/emails/#i-get-no-emails)

### How do I delete my account?

_Profile_ → _My data_ → _Delete account …_ and confirm with your password. This cannot be undone.
[→ Details](/wichtel/en/helpers/privacy/)
