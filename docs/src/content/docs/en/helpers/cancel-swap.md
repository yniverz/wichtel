title: Cancel, give away and swap
description: Cancel until the deadline, then give the shift away on the shift market or to one person, and swap shifts.

:::tip[At a glance]

- **Cancel:** tap the shift → _Cancel_. Works until the deadline shown (usually 48 hours before the
  start).
- **After that:** _Give away …_ → _Offer on the shift market_ or _Offer to a specific person_.
- When giving a shift away **you stay signed up until someone takes over.**
- If you cannot make it at short notice: tell the shift's _Contact_.

:::

## Cancel until the deadline

1. Tap your shift under _Shifts_ (or on the home page).
2. Below the button it says _You can cancel until …_ with date and time.
3. Tap _Cancel_ and confirm.
4. You see _You have cancelled._ If someone is on the waiting list, they move up automatically.

Your festival sets the deadline – usually 48 hours before the start. Individual areas or shifts may
have a different deadline; what is shown at the shift is what counts.

**Without a deadline**, until the shift starts, you can:

- take back a request: _Withdraw request_
- leave the waiting list: _Leave waiting list_
- release a place reserved for you: _Decline_

## After the deadline: give the shift away

Once the deadline has passed, the shift says _Only the shift lead can cancel now._ You can still
pass the shift on to someone else. This also works before the deadline.

1. Tap the shift and then _Give away …_.
2. Choose how to give it away:
   - **_Offer on the shift market_:** Everyone who can take the shift sees the offer.
   - **_Offer to a specific person_:** Enter _Their e-mail address_ and tap _Offer_. The person
     needs a confirmed account.
3. You see _Your offer is online._ Your shift now says _Offered on the shift market_ or _Offered to
   …_.

**You stay signed up until someone takes over.** Only then are you taken off the shift and get an
email "Taken over". If nobody comes, the shift stays yours. With _Withdraw offer_ you take the offer
back.

## Taking over a shift

**From the shift market:** The _Up for grabs_ filter shows offered shifts. The position says
_Someone is giving this place away._ – tap _Take over_. You see _You took over the shift._

**Offered directly:** If someone offers you a shift, you get an email, and the home page shows
"… offers you this shift" under _Waiting for you_. You can:

- _Take over_ – the shift is then yours,
- choose one of your own shifts under _In exchange for …_ and then tap _Take over_ – that is a
  **swap proposal**,
- _Decline_ – the other person gets an email; nothing changes about their shifts.

## Swapping

If someone proposes a swap, you get an email, and the home page shows "… would like to swap" under
_Waiting for you_, with _You give_ and _You get_. Tap _Accept swap_ or _Decline_. Meanwhile your
offered shift says _… suggests a swap – see your home page_.

## When a lead has to approve

Some handovers need a lead's approval first:

- **always** for positions _on request_,
- **after the cancellation deadline**, if your festival or the area is set up that way.

You then see _Takeover requested – a lead will confirm it._, and the shift or the home page shows
_Waiting for a lead_. Both of you get an email "Handover waiting for a lead". **Until the decision,
everything stays as it is.** If the lead declines, you both get the email "Handover did not
happen".

## Common questions

### I am ill and the shift is tomorrow.

Offer the shift on the shift market **and** tell the shift's _Contact_ (shown in the shift
details). The lead can also take you off the shift.

### Why don't I see _Give away …_?

Giving away only works for shifts you are signed up for (not requests or waiting lists), only until
the shift starts – and only if your festival uses the shift market.

### Do I get an email when I cancel?

No. You do get one when a lead takes you off a shift ("Removed") or when someone takes over your
shift.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>What the person taking over must meet</summary>

Whoever takes over needs the required qualifications, must not have another shift at the same time
and must not already be on the shift. Taking over from the shift market also requires that booking
is open for them (booking waves). In a swap, the shift you give away does not count as an overlap.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to my offer if I cancel after all</summary>

If you cancel (or a lead takes you off the shift), your offer ends automatically.

</details>

<details>
<summary>After the shift has started</summary>

Once the shift has started – or as soon as your attendance has been recorded – the shift can no
longer be given away or swapped. Only the lead can help then.

</details>
