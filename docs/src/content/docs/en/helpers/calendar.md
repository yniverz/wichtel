:::tip[At a glance]

- _Profile_ → _Calendar feed_ → _Subscribe in calendar_.
- If that does not work: _Copy link_ and add the link as a subscription in your calendar app.
- New and changed shifts arrive by themselves.
- The link is **personal** – anyone who has it sees your shifts. _Create new link_ makes the old one
  invalid.

:::

## What the calendar shows

- all shifts you are signed up for,
- requests with the prefix _(Requested)_ and reserved places with _(Reserved for you)_,
- for each shift the location or meeting point, position, contact and description.

Waiting lists are not included. If you cancel, the shift disappears at the next update. The texts
are in the language set in your profile.

## Set up the subscription

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open Wichtel in Safari and tap _Profile_ → _Subscribe in calendar_.
2. Confirm that you want to subscribe to the calendar.

If that does not work, tap _Copy link_ and add the link as a calendar subscription in your iPhone's
settings (_Calendar_ → _Accounts_ → _Add Account_ → _Other_ → _Add Subscribed Calendar_). The names
differ slightly depending on the iOS version.

### Android and Google Calendar

The Google Calendar app cannot add subscriptions itself. Here is how:

1. In Wichtel, tap _Profile_ → _Copy link_.
2. Open [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com) in a browser (ideally on a computer) and
   sign in with your Google account.
3. Next to _Other calendars_ tap **+** → _From URL_ → paste the link → _Add calendar_.
4. The calendar appears on your phone after the next sync. If not: turn the new calendar on or
   sync it in the calendar app's settings.

Other calendar apps on Android can often handle _Subscribe in calendar_ directly.

### Outlook

_Copy link_, then in Outlook _Add calendar_ → _Subscribe from web_ (or _From Internet_) and paste
the link.

## Renew the link

If you shared the link by mistake, tap _Create new link_ and confirm. The old link stops working.
Remove the old subscription in your calendar app and set it up again with the new link.

## Common questions

### A change is not in my calendar yet.

How often your calendar checks for changes is decided by the calendar app – with Google Calendar it
can take several hours. When in doubt, what Wichtel shows is what counts.

### Can I edit shifts in the calendar?

No. The subscription is read-only. Signing up, cancelling and swapping only work in Wichtel.
