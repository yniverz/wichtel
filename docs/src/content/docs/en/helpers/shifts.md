:::tip[At a glance]

- **Sign up:** _Shifts_ → pick a day → tap a shift → _Sign up_.
- If it says _Request_, a lead decides. You get an email.
- **Full?** _Join waiting list_ – you move up automatically.
- You can only have one position per shift, and shifts must not overlap.

:::

## Step by step

1. Tap _Shifts_ at the bottom.
2. At the top, pick the **day**. The number next to the day says how many shifts there are that
   day. When you open the page, the app shows the next day with free places. _All days_ shows
   everything at once.
3. Tap a **shift**. It expands and shows the description, _Date_, duration, _Location_, _Meeting
   point_ and _Contact_.
4. Below are the **positions** (for example "Volunteer" and "Shift lead") with free places and
   points.
5. Tap _Sign up_ next to the right position. You see _You are signed up._ and get an email.

Your shifts then appear on the home page under _Your shifts_ and – if you have set it up – in your
[calendar](/wichtel/en/helpers/calendar/).

## Filters

Below the days you can narrow down the list:

- **Area:** _All areas_ or just one area (only shown if there are several).
- **_Free places_:** only shifts with something still free.
- **_Mine_:** only shifts where you are signed up, have sent a request or are on the waiting list.
- **_Up for grabs_:** shifts someone offers on the shift market (only shown if there are any).

If you find nothing, tap _Reset filters_.

## What the list shows

To the right of each shift you see its state:

| Shown                             | Meaning                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _3 free_                          | This many places are still free.                                  |
| _full_                            | All places are taken.                                             |
| _Up for grabs_                    | Full, but someone is giving their place away – you can take over. |
| _Urgent_                          | Help is needed at short notice, often with _+… bonus points_.     |
| _Signed up_                       | You are in.                                                       |
| _Requested_                       | You sent a request; the lead has not decided yet.                 |
| _Waiting list_                    | You are on the waiting list.                                      |
| _Reserved for you_                | Someone from your group is holding a place for you.               |
| _Declined_                        | The lead declined your request.                                   |
| _internal_ (after the area)       | Only visible to people with a role in this area.                  |
| _Also there: …_ (below the title) | These people from your group are on the shift.                    |

Past shifts are shown faded.

## Direct or on request

Each position has a booking mode:

- **Direct:** The button says _Sign up_. You are in right away.
- **On request:** After the places it says _on request_, and the button says _Request_. The lead
  confirms or declines. Until then you see _Requested_. You get an email "Request sent" and later
  "Confirmed" or "Not this time". With _Withdraw request_ you can take the request back until the
  shift starts.

## Qualifications

If a position says _Requires: …_, you need a confirmed
[qualification](/wichtel/en/helpers/qualifications/) for it. If you already have it, there is a ✓
after it. If it is missing, you see the button _Provide now_ instead of _Sign up_. _Nice to have:
…_ is just a hint – you can sign up anyway.

## Waiting list

If a position is full, you see _Join waiting list_ (if your festival uses waiting lists).

- You then see _Waiting list_ and _No. 2 on the waiting list_ (or whatever your place is).
- When a place becomes free, the first person on the list moves up **automatically** and gets an
  email "You moved up". You do not need to do anything.
- For positions _on request_, your waiting-list place turns into a request when you move up, and
  the lead still has to confirm it.
- With _Leave waiting list_ you are no longer waiting.

## Overlaps and breaks

You cannot be on two shifts at the same time. If a shift overlaps with one where you are signed up,
have sent a request or have a reserved place, it says _Overlaps with one of your shifts_ instead of
a button. Some festivals also require a minimum break between two shifts – then a shift that starts
too soon afterwards also counts as an overlap.

## Common questions

### Can I sign up for two positions on the same shift?

No. You have one position per shift. To switch, cancel and then sign up for the other position.

### Why can't I see some shifts?

Internal shifts are only visible to people with a role in the area. Also check whether a filter is
active.

### Do I get a reminder?

Yes, by default 24 hours before the start by email. The organisers can change the timing or turn
reminders off.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>A link in an email opens the shift directly</summary>

Links in emails (for example from an urgent call) jump to the right day and expand the shift right
away.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am on a waiting list but now have another shift at the same time</summary>

Waiting lists do not block other shifts. If a place becomes free while you are already signed up
somewhere else at the same time, you are skipped and stay on the list.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My request was declined</summary>

You then see _Declined_. You can still request again – but best ask the lead first why it did not
work out. Or look for another shift.

</details>
