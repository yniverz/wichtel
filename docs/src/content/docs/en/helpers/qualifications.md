:::tip[At a glance]

- _Profile_ → _Manage qualifications_ → _Provide_ (or _Provide now_ in the shift).
- Tick the box and/or upload a proof → _Submit for review_.
- Only once it says _confirmed_ can you sign up for positions that require it.

:::

## What are qualifications for?

Some positions need a qualification, for example a food hygiene training, a driving licence or a
first-aid course. The shift then says _Requires: …_. Without a confirmed qualification you cannot
sign up there, cannot join the waiting list and cannot take the shift over.

_Nice to have: …_, on the other hand, is just a hint – you can sign up without it.

## Step by step

1. Tap _Profile_ → _Manage qualifications_. Or tap _Provide now_ in the shift – then you land
   directly at the right qualification.
2. Tap _Provide_ next to the qualification.
3. Depending on the qualification, you:
   - tick _I confirm that I have this qualification._,
   - upload a _Proof (PDF or photo, max. 10 MB)_ – PDF, JPG, PNG and WebP are allowed,
   - or may choose either.
4. Under _Note_ you can add a comment (optional).
5. Tap _Submit for review_. You see _Thanks! Your proof will be reviewed._

Even if you only tick the box, someone looks at it before it counts.

## The status

| Shown          | Meaning                                                                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _not provided_ | You have not submitted anything yet.                                         |
| _under review_ | Your proof is being reviewed. Please wait.                                   |
| _confirmed_    | All good. If it says _valid until …_, the qualification expires on that day. |
| _declined_     | Not accepted. _Reviewer’s note: …_ usually says why.                         |
| _expired_      | The validity has run out.                                                    |

For _declined_ and _expired_, tap _Provide again_ and submit your proof once more.

## Common questions

### Who sees my document?

Only the people who review proofs. For some qualifications it also says _The proof is deleted after
the review._

### I have the qualification, but the review is taking a while.

Talk to the lead of your area. They can review your proof or add the qualification for you
directly.

### I see nothing under qualifications.

If it says _No qualifications are set up._, your festival does not need any.
