:::tip[At a glance]

- **Sign up:** _Sign up as a volunteer_ → fill in your details → _Create account_ → open the link in
  the email.
- **No email?** Check your spam folder, log in, tap _Resend email_.
- **Forgot your password?** Tap _Forgot your password?_ on the login page.
- **Change your details:** _Profile_ → _Personal details_.

:::

## Sign up

1. Open your festival's page and tap _Sign up as a volunteer_. On the login page you can also use
   _No account yet?_ → _Sign up_.
2. Fill in _First name_, _Last name_, _Email address_, _Mobile number_ and _Password_. The password
   needs at least 10 characters. Some festivals ask a few more things here.
3. Tap _Create account_. You see _Almost there!_.
4. Open the email "Please confirm your email address" and tap the link inside. The link is valid
   for 48 hours and also works on another device.
5. You see _Your email address is confirmed. Welcome!_ Tap _Log in_ and log in with your email
   address and password. If you came through an invitation link, you then land right there.

If it says _Registration is currently closed_, the festival is not taking on new volunteers right
now. Check back later or ask the organisers.

## No confirmation email

Without a confirmed address you can log in, but you only see the notice _Please confirm your email
address_. Here is how to get further:

1. **Check your spam folder.** The email sometimes takes a few minutes.
2. **Send it again:** Log in and tap _Resend email_. Only the link in the newest email counts;
   older links stop working.
3. **Check the address:** The notice shows where the email went. If you made a typo, sign up again
   with the correct address or ask the organisers to correct it.
4. **Ask the organisers:** If nothing arrives at all, write via _Contact_ at the bottom of the app.
   The organisers can mark your address as confirmed by hand.

If tapping the link shows _This confirmation link is invalid or has expired_, it is older than 48
hours or there is a newer one. Log in and have a new email sent.

## Log in

_Log in_ → email address and password → _Log in_. You stay logged in on that device until you log
out. On a phone, _Log out_ is at the very bottom of your _Profile_; on a computer it is at the top
right.

After several wrong attempts you see _Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes._ Wait a little
and try again – or reset your password.

## Forgot password

1. Tap _Forgot your password?_ on the login page.
2. Enter your email address and tap _Send link_.
3. Open the link in the email "Reset your password". It is valid for **one hour**.
4. Enter a new password and tap _Save password_. Then log in with the new password.

For security reasons the page always says _If an account exists for this address, an email with a
link is on its way_ – even if the address does not exist. If nothing arrives, check your spam
folder and the spelling of the address.

Resetting your password logs you out on all other devices.

## Profile

Under _Profile_ you find:

- **_Personal details_:** _First name_, _Last name_, _Mobile number_ and _Language_. The shift lead
  needs your mobile number to reach you at short notice.
- **_More details_:** questions your festival asks in addition (for example about food). If the home
  page says _Please complete your details in your profile._, something required is still missing
  here.
- **_Change password_:** with your current password. Your other devices are logged out.
- Also: [_Manage qualifications_](/wichtel/en/helpers/qualifications/),
  [_Group_](/wichtel/en/helpers/group/), [_Calendar feed_](/wichtel/en/helpers/calendar/) and
  [_My data_](/wichtel/en/helpers/privacy/).

Your **email address** is shown at the top (_Signed in as …_). You cannot change it yourself – ask
the organisers. Your old address then gets a notice.

## Language and dark mode

- **Language:** Tap **DE** or **EN** at the top, or choose it in your profile under _Language_. The
  setting also applies to your emails.
- **Light or dark:** The app follows the setting of your phone or computer automatically. There is
  no separate switch.

## Common questions

### Do I have to sign up again every year?

No. Your account stays and also works for the next festival year. Points, goodies and groups,
however, always apply to one festival year only.

### I signed up with an address that already has an account.

Then instead of the confirmation you get an email "You already have an account" with a link to set
a new password.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>The organisers sent me a reset link</summary>

If the reset email does not arrive, the organisers can create a link for you and hand it to you in
person or send it to your address. This link is also valid for one hour. Setting a new password via
such a link also confirms your email address.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I get no emails at all, not even a confirmation</summary>

Some festivals use Wichtel without sending any emails. Then you do not need to confirm your address
and go straight into the app after signing up. _Forgot your password?_ does not work in that case –
contact the organisers instead.

</details>
