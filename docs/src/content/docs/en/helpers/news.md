:::tip[At a glance]

- Tap _News_ at the bottom. The number on the menu item shows how many posts are new.
- _Pinned_ posts are at the top, the newest below.
- The newest posts also appear on the home page.
- Some posts also arrive by email.

:::

## Where do I find the news?

- **_News_** in the menu: all posts that concern you. Open long posts with _Read more_.
- **Home page:** the most important (pinned) and newest posts, with _All news →_.

## The counter

The number on the _News_ menu item counts the posts published since you last opened the news. As
soon as you open _News_, everything counts as read and the number disappears.

## Pinned

The organisers can **pin** important posts. They are always shown first with _Pinned_ – sometimes
only until a certain date. The newest follow below. If a post was changed later, it shows _updated
…_.

## Who are the posts from?

From the festival management and from the leads of the areas. You see a post if it concerns you:

- posts to **everyone** (some can even be read publicly without an account),
- posts to **all volunteers** of this festival year – for these you need at least one shift,
- posts to an **area** or a **shift** where you are signed up, have sent a request or have a
  reserved place,
- posts to the **crew** or a particular role, if you have one.

If you cancel a shift, the posts to that shift and its area disappear from your list again. Waiting
lists do not count for this.

If the organisers also wrote a post in English and you have English set, you see the English
version.

## By email

The organisers can also send a post by email to everyone it concerns. The email has the post's title
as its subject and a link to the news. Later changes to the post are not sent again – when in doubt,
what the app shows is what counts.
