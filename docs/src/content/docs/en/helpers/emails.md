---
title: Emails from Wichtel
description: Which emails you get and when – from booking to reminders to swaps – and what to do if none arrive.
---

:::tip[At a glance]

- You get an email when something happens to **your** shifts: booking, change, cancellation,
  moving up, swap – and by default a reminder 24 hours before.
- Emails come in the language set in your _Profile_.
- **Nothing arrives?** Check your spam folder and look in your _Profile_ to see which address you
  are signed in with.

:::

The subject lines below are the default texts. Your festival can change the texts.

## Account

| Subject                              | When                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Please confirm your email address    | after signing up and on _Resend email_ (link valid for 48 hours)              |
| You already have an account          | when someone tries to sign up again with your address                         |
| Reset your password                  | after _Forgot your password?_ (link valid for one hour)                       |
| Your e-mail address at … was changed | to your **old** address when the organisers changed it                        |
| Your … account will be deleted soon  | two weeks before an account that has not been used for a long time is deleted |

## Shifts

| Subject              | When                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You are signed up: … | you signed up, accepted a reserved place or took over a shift                                 |
| Request sent: …      | you sent a request for a position _on request_                                                |
| Confirmed: …         | the lead confirmed your request                                                               |
| Not this time: …     | the lead declined your request                                                                |
| You moved up: …      | a place became free and you moved up from the waiting list                                    |
| You were added: …    | a lead signed you up                                                                          |
| Removed: …           | a lead took you off the shift                                                                 |
| Changed: …           | the time, location or meeting point of your shift changed                                     |
| Cancelled: …         | the shift is not taking place (also if you were on the waiting list)                          |
| Reminder: …          | before your shift – by default 24 hours before; the organisers can change this or turn it off |
| Urgently needed: …   | a shift needs help at short notice, you are free at that time and meet the requirements       |

Cancelling yourself, withdrawing a request or leaving a waiting list does not trigger an email.

## Group, giving away and swapping

| Subject                        | When                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Place reserved for you: …      | someone from your group included you in a shift            |
| Reservation expired: …         | you did not accept the reserved place in time              |
| … offers you a shift           | someone wants to give you a shift directly                 |
| … suggests a swap              | someone wants to swap your offered shift for one of theirs |
| Handover waiting for a lead: … | a handover has been agreed, a lead still has to approve it |
| Taken over: …                  | someone took over your shift – you are off it              |
| Handover did not happen: …     | a handover or swap was declined                            |

## News

Posts from the organisers or your areas can also arrive by email. The subject is the post's title.
More under [News](/wichtel/en/helpers/news/).

## I get no emails

1. **Check your spam or promotions folder** and mark the sender as safe.
2. **Check the address:** At the top of your _Profile_ it says _Signed in as …_. If the address is
   wrong, only the organisers can change it – write via _Contact_ at the bottom of the app.
3. **Wait a few minutes.** Emails are sent one after another; if sending does not work right away,
   it is retried automatically.
4. **Ask the organisers.** They can see whether emails were sent to you and can resend stuck ones.

Important: everything in the emails is also in the app – your shifts under _Shifts_ → _Mine_ and on
the home page, offers and reserved places under _Waiting for you_.

## Special cases

<details>
<summary>I get no emails at all from my festival</summary>

Some festivals use Wichtel without sending any emails. Then you need to check everything in the
app. Ask the organisers if in doubt.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The email is in German, I want English</summary>

Set English under _Profile_ → _Language_ (or with **EN** at the top). New emails then come in
English.

</details>
