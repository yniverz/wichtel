:::tip[At a glance]

- **Download:** _Profile_ → _My data_ → _Download my data_.
- **Delete:** _Profile_ → _My data_ → _Delete account …_ → password → _Delete account for good_.
- Your personal data is deleted. Past shifts and points remain **without your name** for
  statistics.
- Unused accounts are deleted automatically after a while – with a warning by email.

:::

## Download your data

1. Tap _Profile_ and scroll to _My data_.
2. Tap _Download my data_.

You get a file with everything stored about you: profile and further details, shifts (also from
earlier years) with attendance, points, goodies, qualifications, groups, handovers, roles, sign-ins
and a log of your actions. Your password is not in the file.

The file is a JSON file – a text file you can open with any text editor.

## Delete your account

:::caution
Deleting cannot be undone. Your upcoming shifts are cancelled right away.
:::

1. _Profile_ → _My data_ → _Delete account …_.
2. Enter your _Password_.
3. Tap _Delete account for good_. You see _Your account has been deleted. Thanks for your help!_

**What is deleted:**

- name, email address and mobile number,
- your further details from the profile,
- your qualifications including uploaded proofs,
- your group membership and your roles,
- all sign-ins on your devices.

**What happens to your shifts and goodies:**

- Upcoming shifts, requests, waiting-list places and reserved places are cancelled. Whoever is on
  the waiting list moves up.
- Running offers to give away or swap end.
- Goodies you chose but have not picked up yet are cancelled.
- **Past shifts, attendance, points and goodies already picked up remain** – but without your name,
  as "Deleted account". That way the festival's numbers stay correct.

Afterwards you can sign up again with the same email address – as a new account without your old
shifts and points.

## Automatic deletion after inactivity

So that no data is kept longer than necessary, Wichtel deletes accounts that have not been used for
a long time. Your festival sets the period (by default 24 months); it can also turn automatic
deletion off.

- "Used" means: logging in or opening the app, or a shift you had. Whatever happened last counts.
- **Two weeks before**, you get an email "Your … account will be deleted soon".
- **If you want to stay,** just log in once before then. That is all you need to do.
- If you do nothing, your account is deleted – exactly as described above: shifts and points remain
  without your name.

## Common questions

### I forgot my password and want to delete my account.

Reset your password first (see [Forgot password](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/#forgot-password)) –
or write to the organisers. On request they can also delete your account themselves or send you
your data.

### Where does it say exactly what is stored and why?

In your festival's privacy policy. It is usually linked at the very bottom of the app under
_Privacy_.
