title: I cannot sign up
description: Every reason why signing up does not work – sorted by what the app shows – and what you can do in each case.
---

:::tip[At a glance]

- Tap the shift and read what is shown **instead of the _Sign up_ button** – that is usually the
  reason.
- Most common reasons: booking not open for you yet, overlap with another shift, missing
  qualification, position full.
- If none of this helps: write to the shift's _Contact_ or to the organisers via _Contact_.

:::

## What is shown instead of the button

### "Booking opens …" or "Booking currently closed"

Your festival releases shifts in **booking waves**, for example first for the crew, then for
volunteers from last year, then for everyone. Booking is not open for you yet.

- _Booking opens …_ tells you when it starts for you. The top of the page then often also says
  _Booking opens for you on …_.
- _Booking currently closed_ means: no wave is open for you right now, and none is announced.
- If you received an **invitation link**, open it while you are logged in. You see _Invitation
  accepted: …_, and you can book as soon as that wave is open.

### "Overlaps with one of your shifts"

At the same time you are already signed up for another shift, have sent a request there or have a
reserved place. Some festivals also require a minimum break between two shifts.

**What to do:** Tap the _Mine_ filter and see which shift is in the way. If you prefer the new one,
cancel the old one (see [Cancel and swap](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)).

### "Provide now"

The position needs a qualification (_Requires: …_) that is not confirmed for you yet.

**What to do:** Tap _Provide now_ and submit your proof. Only once the review has confirmed it can
you sign up. If your qualification says _under review_, you still have to wait. More under
[Qualifications](/wichtel/en/helpers/qualifications/).

### "full" and no button

All places are taken.

- If there is _Join waiting list_, join it – you move up automatically.
- If it shows _Up for grabs_ or _Take over_, someone is giving their place away and you can take it
  over right away.
- If you also lack a required qualification, neither the waiting list nor _Take over_ is shown.

### "Request" instead of "Sign up"

Not an error: this position is filled _on request_. Tap _Request_ and the lead decides. Until then
you see _Requested_.

### "Declined"

The lead declined your request for this shift. The app does not offer you to sign up for this shift
again. Talk to the lead if you have questions.

### The shift is faded and has no button

The shift has already started or is over. You can only sign up before the start.

### I cannot see the shift at all

- A filter is active: tap _Reset filters_ or choose _All days_.
- The shift is **internal** and only visible to people with a role in the area.
- _No shifts have been published yet_ or _There is no volunteer season running right now_: the
  organisers have not published anything yet.

### I only see "Please confirm your email address"

Without a confirmed email address you cannot book shifts. See
[No confirmation email](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/#no-confirmation-email).

## Messages after tapping

Sometimes something changes while you have the page open – then you get a message after tapping:

| Message                                                          | What happened and what you can do                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _All places are taken._                                          | Someone was faster. Reload the page, use the waiting list.                                                        |
| _This overlaps with another shift._                              | See "Overlaps with one of your shifts" above.                                                                     |
| _This position needs a qualification that is not confirmed yet._ | See "Provide now" above.                                                                                          |
| _Booking for this shift is not open right now._                  | See "Booking opens …" above.                                                                                      |
| _There already is a booking for this shift._                     | You are already signed up, have a request or are on the waiting list for this shift. Only one position per shift. |
| _This shift has already started._                                | You can only sign up before the start.                                                                            |
| _There are still free places – sign up directly._                | You wanted to join the waiting list, but something is free again. Reload and tap _Sign up_.                       |
| _This does not exist (anymore)._                                 | The shift was deleted or is not visible to you. Reload the page.                                                  |
| _The reservation has expired._                                   | A place reserved for you has lapsed. See whether anything is still free.                                          |
| _It is very busy right now. Please try again in a few seconds._  | Very many people are booking at once. Wait a moment and tap again.                                                |
| _Something went wrong. Please try again._                        | Try again. If it happens again, write to the organisers.                                                          |

## When signing up with your group

| Message                                          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _This position only takes individual requests._  | Positions _on request_ only work one by one.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| _There are not enough free places for everyone._ | Take fewer people along or look for another shift.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| _Choose at least one person from your group._    | Tick at least one person.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| _This does not work for everyone:_ with a list   | It does not work for the people named (cannot see this shift, cannot book yet, is already signed up, lacks the required qualification, has another shift at the same time). **Nobody** was signed up. Untick these people and try again. |

## Background

When you sign up, Wichtel always checks the same things in this order: Is the shift visible to you?
Is booking open for you? Has the shift not started yet? Are you not on this shift yet? Is a place
free? Do you have the required qualifications? Is there no overlap? The shift lead can also sign
you up themselves – and may knowingly override some rules when doing so. So if you are stuck, just
ask.
