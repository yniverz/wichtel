Everything Wichtel can do – sorted by role. The short version is in the
[README on GitHub](https://github.com/yniverz/wichtel). Terms are explained in the
[glossary](/wichtel/en/reference/glossary/).

## For volunteers

- **[Sign-up](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/)** with e-mail confirmation (skipped without a mail
  server), log-in, forgotten password, profile. Additional details are only asked for when they are
  needed.
- **[Booking shifts on your phone](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/)**: programme by day (it opens the next
  day with free places), filters (area, free places, mine, up for grabs), signing up with one tap.
  Overlaps and minimum breaks are prevented
  ([Why can’t I sign up?](/wichtel/en/helpers/cannot-book/)).
- **[Cancelling](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)** up to a deadline; after that via the shift market
  or a lead.
- **[Waiting list](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/)** for full positions, moving up automatically.
- **[Shift market and swaps](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)**: offer your own shift to everyone or
  to one person by e-mail; that person can take it over or propose one of their own shifts in exchange.
- **[Groups](/wichtel/en/helpers/group/)** via invitation link: see who helps when and sign up
  together – the others get a reserved place that expires.
- **[Points and goodies](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/)**: points for every shift worked, choose
  goodies yourself; mandatory goodies (e.g. the free ticket) are redeemed first automatically or
  refunded.
- **[News](/wichtel/en/helpers/news/)**: messages from the leads that concern you, with an unread
  counter; the newest also on the home page.
- **[Personal QR code](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/)** for check-in and goodie pickup.
- **[Qualifications](/wichtel/en/helpers/qualifications/)**: provide proof (tick a box or upload a
  document).
- **[Places with plan and map](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/)**: meeting points on your own site plan,
  on OpenStreetMap (only loaded after consent) and as a link to Google Maps/Apple Maps.
- **[Calendar feed](/wichtel/en/helpers/calendar/)** (iCal) with all your shifts.
- **[E-mails](/wichtel/en/helpers/emails/)** on booking, change, cancellation, moving up from the
  waiting list, and as a reminder before the shift.
- **[My data](/wichtel/en/helpers/privacy/)**: download everything stored, delete your account
  yourself.
- [German/English, light/dark mode](/wichtel/en/helpers/account/).

## For leads

- **[Overview](/wichtel/en/leads/dashboard/)**: staffing in percent, heatmap by area and day, shifts
  understaffed next, today’s attendance, open requests – refreshes every minute.
- **[Shifts](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/)** with several [positions](/wichtel/en/leads/positions/) (own
  number of places, booking direct or on request), public or internal, as a
  [series](/wichtel/en/leads/series/) (“every Fri/Sat 18–22 and 22–02”), duplicate.
- **[Requirements](/wichtel/en/leads/qualifications/)** per position: qualifications as required or
  “nice to have”.
- **[Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)**: add people (rules can only be overridden with a special
  permission), decide [requests and handovers](/wichtel/en/leads/requests/) in one combined view,
  tick off attendance.
- **[Urgent call](/wichtel/en/leads/urgent/)**: e-mail with a direct link to everyone who fits and has
  time, optionally with bonus points.
- **[News](/wichtel/en/leads/news/)** for areas, shifts, roles, the crew, all volunteers or public
  (visible without an account); optionally also by e-mail to everyone concerned (replaces the group
  e-mail). Important posts can be pinned to the top, also until a date. Leads only write to their own
  areas.
- **[Print views](/wichtel/en/leads/print/)**: shift plan per area/day and crew list per shift with a
  tick-off column.
- **[Desk & check-in](/wichtel/en/leads/desk/)**: scan the QR code, check in today’s shifts, hand out
  goodies.

## For the organisers

- **[Editions](/wichtel/en/organizers/editions/)** (festival years) with an
  **[area tree](/wichtel/en/organizers/areas/)** of any depth; **copy an edition** with all times
  shifted by whole days.
- **[Roles](/wichtel/en/organizers/roles/)** freely configurable, assigned per area and inherited by
  sub-areas. Nobody can grant more permissions than they have themselves
  ([all permissions](/wichtel/en/reference/permissions/)).
- **[Points rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/points/)** per shift and/or hour, inherited instance → area →
  position, night and last-minute bonus, manual corrections with a reason.
- **[Goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)** with price, options, area restriction, maximum,
  contingent, stock and pickup place (a place with map/site plan plus a note such as opening hours;
  otherwise the volunteer desk).
- **[Booking waves](/wichtel/en/organizers/waves/)**: crew first, then returning volunteers,
  invitation links, then everyone.
- **[Profile fields](/wichtel/en/organizers/fields/)** freely configurable (at sign-up, in the profile
  or when choosing goodies).
- **[Appearance](/wichtel/en/organizers/appearance/)**: name, tagline, colours with contrast check,
  logo, background, favicon.
- **[E-mail templates](/wichtel/en/organizers/mail/)** customisable per language.
- **[E-mail delivery](/wichtel/en/organizers/mail/)**: test mail to yourself, resend stuck or failed
  mails, log of all mails of the last 30 days (recipient, subject, state).
- **[Editing accounts](/wichtel/en/organizers/people/)**: admins correct name, e-mail, phone, language
  and profile fields; with a new address, the old one receives a notice. If a confirmation mail does
  not arrive, the address can be marked as confirmed by hand. People set their passwords themselves.
- **[Audit log](/wichtel/en/organizers/audit/)** of all admin actions.
- **[Data protection](/wichtel/en/organizers/privacy/)**: volunteers download their data themselves
  and delete their account; admins can do both for requests by post. Deleted accounts lose all
  personal data; shifts and points remain anonymously for statistics. Inactive accounts are anonymised
  automatically after an adjustable period (with a warning by mail); IP addresses in the audit log are
  removed after another period.
- **[Legal notice and privacy policy](/wichtel/en/organizers/privacy/)** are maintained in the admin
  area, not in the code. For the privacy policy there is a template that fits the installation
  (profile fields, retention periods, maps, AI access). It is no substitute for legal advice: passages
  in [square brackets] have to be completed.
- **[Delivery](/wichtel/en/organizers/mail/)**: mails that do not go out are visible in the admin area
  and can be resent or discarded.
- **[AI assistants](/wichtel/en/organizers/ai/)** (e.g. Claude) with the permissions of the respective
  person – see also [AI assistant for leads](/wichtel/en/leads/ai/).

How it all fits together is described in the [concept](/wichtel/en/reference/concept/).
