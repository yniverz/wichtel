---
title: Glossary
description: Every term you come across in Wichtel – explained briefly, with a link to the detailed help.
---

The terms are spelled the way the app shows them in English. Every entry can be linked directly,
e.g. `/wichtel/en/reference/glossary/#shift`.

### Admin

An account with the _Instance admin_ switch (shown with the _Admin_ badge in lists). Admins manage
settings, editions, roles and other admins and have every permission everywhere. This is not a role
but a property of the account, and it applies to all editions.
More: [Permissions](/wichtel/en/reference/permissions/#admin-only), [People](/wichtel/en/organizers/people/)

### Admin area

The part of the app for leads and admins (menu item _Admin_). What you see there depends on your own
permissions.
More: [The admin area](/wichtel/en/leads/admin-area/)

### Advance

A goodie with _Advance_ can already be chosen with pending points, e.g. the T-shirt before the first
shift.
More: [Setting up goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### AI assistant

An assistant such as Claude that can be connected to Wichtel. It acts with exactly the permissions of
the person who connected it; that person needs the permission _Connect an AI assistant_.
More: [AI assistant for leads](/wichtel/en/leads/ai/), [Setting up AI assistants](/wichtel/en/organizers/ai/)

### Area

A part of the festival structure, nested as deeply as you like (e.g. working group → area → team).
Areas belong to an edition. Roles, deadlines and points rules on an area also apply to its sub-areas.
More: [Areas](/wichtel/en/organizers/areas/)

### Attendance

Whether someone turned up for a shift: _present_, _no-show_ or _open_. Leads set it in the staffing
list of a shift or through [check-in](#check-in). Attendance is what turns pending points into
confirmed ones.
More: [Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)

### Audit log

The unchangeable list of all changes made by leads and admins – and of some that volunteers make
themselves (e.g. swaps, groups, deleting their account): who, when, what. Anyone with the permission
_View audit log_ can read it.
More: [Audit log](/wichtel/en/organizers/audit/)

### Bonus points

Extra points on top of the basic rule: _Night bonus_ for night shifts, _Last-minute bonus_ for
shifts booked at short notice, and bonus points for an [urgent call](#urgent-call).
More: [Points rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/points/)

### Booking

The link between a person and a [position](#position) in a shift. Volunteers see these states:

- _Signed up_ – the place is yours.
- _Requested_ – a lead still has to confirm your request.
- _Declined_ – a lead declined the request.
- _Waiting list_ – the position is full; when a place becomes free, you move up automatically.
- _Reserved for you_ – someone in your [group](#group) kept a place for you; accept or decline it.

Anyone who cancels disappears from the staffing list. In the admin area, open cases are shown next to
the person as _Request_, _Waiting list_ or _reserved_.
More: [Signing up for shifts](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/), [Staffing](/wichtel/en/leads/staffing/)

### Booking wave

A booking wave opens shifts to a target group at a set time: e.g. first the [crew](#crew), then
returning volunteers, people with an [invitation link](#invitation-link), then everyone. Without waves,
booking is always open to everyone.
More: [Booking waves](/wichtel/en/organizers/waves/)

### Calendar feed

A secret link that puts your shifts into your phone's calendar automatically. You find it in your
_Profile_.
More: [Calendar feed](/wichtel/en/helpers/calendar/)

### Cancel

Removing yourself from a shift. You can do this until the [cancellation deadline](#cancellation-deadline);
after that only through the [shift market](#shift-market) or a lead.
More: [Cancelling and swapping](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)

### Cancellation deadline

How many hours before the start you can still cancel yourself (default 48). It can be set for the whole
instance, per area and per shift.
More: [Booking rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/booking-rules/)

### Check-in

Confirming that someone is there for their shift – by scanning their [personal code](#your-code) at
_Desk & check-in_ or by ticking them off in the staffing list. Possible from midnight on the day of the
shift. Check-in confirms the points, and any due [mandatory goodies](#mandatory-goodie) are redeemed
immediately.
More: [Desk & check-in](/wichtel/en/leads/desk/), [On site](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/)

### Contingent

The _Self-service contingent_ limits how often volunteers can choose a goodie themselves (e.g. 50
items). The desk can always hand out more.
More: [Setting up goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### Crew

Everyone who has any [role](#role) in the current edition. Booking waves and news can be aimed at the
crew.
More: [Booking waves](/wichtel/en/organizers/waves/)

### Desk & check-in

A page in the admin area for the volunteer desk and the goodie counter: scan a personal code or search
for a person, check in today's shifts, hand out goodies, correct points.
More: [Desk & check-in](/wichtel/en/leads/desk/)

### Edition

One festival year (German _Jahrgang_). Areas, shifts, points, goodies, waves and role assignments always
belong to exactly one edition; volunteers always see the current one. A new edition can be copied from
the previous one.
More: [Editions](/wichtel/en/organizers/editions/)

### Goodie

A reward that volunteers trade their points for – e.g. a T-shirt, drink tokens or the free ticket. A
goodie can have options (e.g. sizes), a maximum per person, a [contingent](#contingent) and a pickup
place. Chosen goodies show as _ready for pickup_, handed-out ones as _picked up_.
More: [Points and goodies](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/), [Setting up goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### Group

Friends who volunteer together. In a group you see who helps when and can sign up together (_Sign up
with group_): you are signed up straight away, the others get a reserved place that expires.
More: [Volunteering as a group](/wichtel/en/helpers/group/)

### Handover

When a shift changes hands through the [shift market](#shift-market) or a [swap](#swap). Depending on
the settings, a lead must approve handovers after the deadline (_Waiting for a lead_); for positions
_on request_ always.
More: [Deciding requests](/wichtel/en/leads/requests/), [Booking rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/booking-rules/)

### Instance

One Wichtel installation with its settings, its appearance and all accounts. Accounts belong to the
whole instance and are kept across editions.
More: [Settings](/wichtel/en/organizers/settings/)

### Invitation link

A link for joining a [group](#group) or for being let in by a [booking wave](#booking-wave) aimed at
_People with the invitation link_. Groups also have an _Invite code_.
More: [Group](/wichtel/en/helpers/group/), [Booking waves](/wichtel/en/organizers/waves/)

### Lead

Umbrella term for everyone who has admin permissions for areas through a [role](#role), e.g. an area
lead or a shift lead. What a lead may do exactly is defined by their role.
More: [I lead an area](/wichtel/en/leads/)

### Mandatory goodie

A goodie that is redeemed automatically as soon as there are enough confirmed points – e.g. the free
ticket. If there are several, the set order applies. Mandatory goodies cannot be undone, but they can
be refunded if that is allowed (see [refund](#refund)).
More: [Points and goodies](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/), [Setting up goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### Minimum break

How many minutes must lie between two of your shifts at least (default 0). Overlaps are always
blocked.
More: [Booking rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/booking-rules/)

### News

Posts by the organisers and leads for particular audiences, in the _News_ menu. Unread posts are
counted, important ones pinned to the top. If wanted, they also go out by e-mail to everyone
concerned.
More: [Reading news](/wichtel/en/helpers/news/), [Writing news](/wichtel/en/leads/news/)

### Permission

A single capability in the admin area, e.g. _Create and edit shifts_. Permissions are never granted
one by one but through [roles](#role).
More: [Permissions](/wichtel/en/reference/permissions/)

### Place

A meeting point or work location with a pin on the [site plan](#site-plan) and/or the map. Shifts
refer to places so that volunteers find their way; links open the place in Google Maps or Apple Maps.
More: [Places & site plan](/wichtel/en/organizers/places/)

### Points

Every shift you work earns points, which you trade for goodies. _Confirmed points_ have been credited
(after check-in) and can be spent. _Pending_ points come from booked shifts that are not confirmed yet
and are only a preview. Points are valid only in their edition.
More: [Points and goodies](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/), [Points rules](/wichtel/en/organizers/points/)

### Position

A job within a shift with its own number of places, e.g. “4× Volunteer, 1× Shift lead”. Each position
has a booking mode (_direct_ or _on request_), its own points and its own
[qualification](#qualification) requirements.
More: [Positions](/wichtel/en/leads/positions/)

### Profile field

An extra question for volunteers, e.g. T-shirt size or diet. It is asked at sign-up, only in the
profile, or only when choosing certain goodies.
More: [Profile fields](/wichtel/en/organizers/fields/)

### Qualification

A proof such as food-hygiene training or a driving licence – by ticking a box or uploading a document,
reviewed by a lead. On a position, a qualification is either _required_ (no sign-up without it) or
_nice to have_ (just a hint). States: _not provided_, _under review_, _confirmed_, _declined_,
_expired_.
More: [Providing qualifications](/wichtel/en/helpers/qualifications/), [Reviewing qualifications](/wichtel/en/leads/qualifications/)

### Refund

For mandatory goodies you already have (e.g. a ticket you bought), you can choose _I already have
one_. The points stay spent; instead of handing it out, the goodie shows _refund pending_ until the desk
uses _Mark refunded_. The money itself is paid back outside Wichtel.
More: [Points and goodies](/wichtel/en/helpers/points-goodies/), [Setting up goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### Request

For positions with booking _on request_, volunteers tap _Request_ instead of _Sign up_. A lead approves
or declines; until then the shift shows as _Requested_. Leads find all open requests under _Requests_.
More: [Signing up for shifts](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/), [Deciding requests](/wichtel/en/leads/requests/)

### Role

A bundle of [permissions](#permission), e.g. _Area lead_ or _Goodie desk_. A role is given to a person
for one edition and one [scope](#scope). Nobody can grant more permissions than they hold there
themselves.
More: [Roles](/wichtel/en/organizers/roles/)

### Scope

What an assigned [role](#role) applies to: the _whole edition_ or one area including all its sub-areas.
In the app the field is called _Applies to_.
More: [Permissions](/wichtel/en/reference/permissions/#scope-and-inheritance)

### Series

Many similar shifts at once, e.g. “Fri and Sat, 18–22 and 22–02 each”. In the admin area: _Create
series_.
More: [Series](/wichtel/en/leads/series/)

### Shift

A period in an area when volunteers are needed – with place, meeting point, description and contact
person. Every shift has one or more [positions](#position).
More: [Signing up for shifts](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/), [Creating shifts](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/)

### Shift market

Where volunteers offer one of their shifts to everyone (_Offer on the shift market_). Anyone who can
take it sees it with the _Up for grabs_ filter and taps _Take over_. You stay signed up until someone
takes over.
More: [Cancelling and swapping](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)

### Site plan

An image of your festival grounds, per edition. The organisers place pins for [places](#place) on it.
More: [Places & site plan](/wichtel/en/organizers/places/)

### Stock

An optional number of items for a goodie, for overview only. Unlike the [contingent](#contingent),
stock blocks nothing.
More: [Setting up goodies](/wichtel/en/organizers/goodies/)

### Swap

Offering a shift directly to a specific person (_Offer to a specific person_). They can take it over or
propose one of their own shifts in exchange; you then accept or decline the swap.
More: [Cancelling and swapping](/wichtel/en/helpers/cancel-swap/)

### Urgent call

An e-mail with a direct link to everyone who fits an understaffed position and is free at that time –
optionally with bonus points. In the app the shift is then marked _Urgent_. At most once an hour per
position.
More: [Urgent call](/wichtel/en/leads/urgent/)

### Visibility

A shift is visible to _all volunteers_ or _only people with a role in this area_ (marked _internal_ in
the app).
More: [Creating shifts](/wichtel/en/leads/shifts/)

### Volunteer desk

The central point on site for check-in and goodies. Its place is shown to volunteers on the home page.
_Volunteer desk_ is also the name of one of the predefined roles.
More: [On site](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/), [Places & site plan](/wichtel/en/organizers/places/)

### Waiting list

When a position is full, you can join its waiting list. When a place becomes free, the person who has
waited longest moves up automatically and gets an e-mail.
More: [Signing up for shifts](/wichtel/en/helpers/shifts/)

### Your code

Every volunteer's personal QR code, found under _Goodies_ → _Show my code_. It is scanned at the
volunteer desk and the goodie counter.
More: [On site](/wichtel/en/helpers/on-site/)
